## K-theoretic Thom polynomials

<div class="wp-content" markdown="0">
There are three different versions of the concept ``K-theoretic fundamental class'':<ul><li>The class of the structure sheaf.</li><li>The class of the structure sheaf of a resolution, pushed forward.</li><li>The Motivic Chern class mC_y, with y=0 substitution.</li></ul>These three concepts only coincide if the variety has mild singularities.

Correspondingly, there are three different concepts for ``K-theoretic Thom polynomial''.
